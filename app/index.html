    Keyboard and mouse

    Tools

    VMove
    AEdit points of a line or path
    B L U PBrush, line, ellipse, pen
    EEraser
    H · hold SpacePan (or drag with the middle mouse button)

    Drawing

    [ ]Brush / eraser size
    ShiftLine: snap to 15° · ellipse: circle
    AltPen: corner point · ellipse: from the centre
    Enter · double-clickFinish a pen path; click its first point to close
    Backspace · EscPen: remove the last point · cancel

    Selection

    Shift-clickAdd or remove a stroke · drag on empty space to select with a box
    Alt-dragCopy while moving
    CtrlA · CtrlDSelect all · none
    ArrowsNudge 1 px · Shift 10 px
    DelDelete (or the point being edited)
    Right-clickMenu for the stroke under the cursor

    Transform and arrange

    ShiftH · ShiftVFlip horizontally · vertically
    Q · WRotate −15° · +15°
    CtrlJDuplicate
    CtrlG · CtrlShiftGGroup · ungroup (double-click a group to edit inside it, Esc to leave)
    Ctrl] · Ctrl[Forward · backward; with Shift: front · back
    XSwitch between main and second colour
    CtrlShift;Snapping on or off
    Ctrl while draggingMove or scale without snapping
    Properties · AlignAlign, space evenly, centre on the page

    View

    Ctrl+wheelZoom at the cursor · wheel pans
    Ctrl0 · Ctrl1Fit · 100%
    Ctrl= · Ctrl-Zoom in · out
    CtrlLLine art view
    TabHide / show both panels

    Edit and files

    CtrlZ · CtrlShiftZUndo · redo (also CtrlY)
    CtrlS · CtrlESave draft · export
    CtrlAltN · W · TNew tab · close tab · reopen a closed tab
    CtrlAltSSave all tabs
    CtrlAlt← →Previous · next tab
    TabsDouble-click renames · drag reorders · Ctrl-click selects several · right-click: save or close several

    Sliders and fields

    Drag a nameAdjust the value · Shift faster · Alt finer
    Type · EnterSet an exact value · Esc cancels
    Double-clickReset a slider or panel width
